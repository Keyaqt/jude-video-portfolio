About me

Creative editing, supported by AI.

I’m Jude Rebamonte, an AI Video Editor focused on creating engaging UGC and VSL content.

I started editing in August 2025 after becoming curious about AI video production and exploring it through hands-on practice. Since then, I’ve focused on creating scroll-stopping content through strong pacing, captions, B-roll, sound design, and AI-assisted visuals.

My process starts with understanding the script first. From there, I decide on the visual direction and choose B-roll that supports the message. I also handle prompting, AI image generation, editing, revisions, and final delivery.

I see AI as part of the creative process, not a replacement for creative judgment. The final result still depends on pacing, storytelling, visual choices, and how well every scene supports the message.

I’m currently looking for a full-time opportunity where I can contribute to UGC, VSL, and other modern video projects while continuing to grow as an AI-focused creative editor.
